Academic Trajectory & Research

K. M. Safin Kamal is a researcher and academic serving as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at East West University, Dhaka.

His primary scholarship centers on developing specialized deep learning models tailored for clinical diagnostic automation. His work spans computer vision and CNN frameworks for micro-cellular classification—including red blood cell hematology for anemia screening, leukemic white blood cell analysis, and bone fracture recognition.

In parallel, Mr. Kamal investigates sustainable computing architectures, formulating greedy task-scheduling algorithms and energy-conscious resource allocation protocols for green cloud environments and blockchain networks.
